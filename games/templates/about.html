ABOUT GDBR

Built for people who love games.

GDBR is a modern game discovery and personal library platform designed to help players explore great games, discover iconic series, revisit Game of the Year titles, and build their own personal collection.

WHAT GDBR OFFERS

Everything in one place

🎮

Game Discovery

Search for games, explore ratings, release information and discover titles from different genres and eras.

⭐

Personal Library

Add your favorite games to My Games and create a personal collection you can easily access anytime.

🏆

Game of the Year

Explore Game of the Year winners and nominees from different years through a dedicated archive.

🔗

Iconic Series

OUR PURPOSE

More than a game list

Our Mission

GDBR aims to make discovering and organizing games simple, enjoyable and visually engaging. Instead of overwhelming users with unnecessary information, the platform focuses on the details that matter most when exploring a game.

The GDBR Vision

Our goal is to create a growing destination where players can discover new experiences, revisit classic games and keep track of the titles that matter to them.

Discover. Play. Remember.

Whether you're searching for your next game, exploring legendary franchises or building your personal collection, GDBR is designed to keep the experience simple and enjoyable.
